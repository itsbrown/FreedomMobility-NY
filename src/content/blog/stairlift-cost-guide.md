## Average prices in our service area

- **Straight stairlifts:** $3,000 – $5,500 installed (most common in newer homes in Henrietta, Webster, or Amherst).
- **Curved stairlifts:** $8,000 – $15,000+ (typical for historic homes in Park Avenue Rochester, Elmwood Village Buffalo, or older Syracuse neighborhoods).
- **Outdoor stairlifts:** $4,500 – $7,000 (popular for homes with exterior steps exposed to NY winters).
- **Heavy-duty models:** Add $1,000–$3,000 for higher weight capacities.

These ranges reflect what families in Western and Central New York actually pay for a professionally measured and installed lift, not a national average copied from a spreadsheet.

## What affects the price?

Factors specific to Western and Central NY homes:

- Staircase length and number of turns (many older homes have L-shaped or spiral stairs).
- Indoor vs outdoor installation (snow load and weatherproofing add cost).
- Power source and battery backup requirements.
- Custom rail colors or seat options.

The only way to know where you land in the range is to measure the stairs. That visit is free.

## Financing and warranties

Most customers in our area finance over 12–60 months. We include an 18-month Gold Star Service Warranty on all new installations, with extended options available.

If you are comparing quotes, ask what is included: rail, chair, install, haul-away of an old lift, and who shows up for service in January.
