## Typical timeline

Most straight indoor installs in our area are done in a few hours, not days. The rail fastens to the stair treads — not the wall — so we are not opening plaster.

## What we need from you

- Clear the staircase
- A nearby outlet (we will tell you if a new receptacle is required)
- Someone home who can try the lift before we leave

## After we leave

You should be able to use the stairs the same day. We walk through charging, the swivel, and what to do in a power outage.
