Ramp Buying Guide for Upstate New York Homes

How to choose a ramp for a Rochester, Buffalo, or Syracuse home. Portable, modular, and permanent options with local weather and code notes.

Updated August 27, 2026 · Rochester · Buffalo · Syracuse

Types of ramps we install

  • Portable / folding ramps: Best for temporary use or rentals. Easy to store and move. Useful for Rochester apartments or short-term recovery.
  • Modular aluminum ramps: Quick to install, adjustable, and removable. Popular in Buffalo for homes with changing needs.
  • Permanent wood or concrete ramps: Most durable and code-friendly for long-term use in Syracuse and surrounding towns.

Local considerations

Upstate New York weather means ramps need traction (we specify non-slip surfaces) and drainage. Salt, freeze-thaw, and lake-effect snow punish cheap plywood. Many older homes have limited yard or porch space, so custom layouts are common.

Read the winter accessibility guide if snow is the main reason you are shopping.

ADA guidelines and local codes

For residential use, we follow best practices for slope (1:12 ratio is the target) and width. Commercial or public buildings have stricter requirements. We will tell you if a permit is likely before you spend money.
