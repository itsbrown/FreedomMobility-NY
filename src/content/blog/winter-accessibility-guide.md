Practical advice for staying independent during harsh Rochester, Buffalo, and Syracuse winters.

## Outdoor equipment that withstands NY winters

Our outdoor stairlifts and vertical platform lifts are specified for lake-effect snow, freezing temperatures, and ice. Key features include:

- Weatherproof covers and heated rails or seats
- Corrosion-resistant hardware for salt and moisture
- Battery backup that still performs in the cold

If the lift sits outside, it has to be an outdoor model. That is not a marketing line — indoor gear fails outside.

## Ramp considerations for snow

Permanent ramps should drain and use non-slip decking. We recommend heated options or modular aluminum that you can clear with a shovel. Portable ramps are fine for a season, then store them dry.

## Maintenance before the first storm

Regular service matters more here than in a mild climate. We offer pre-season inspections for equipment in Rochester, Buffalo, and Syracuse so a dead battery does not strand someone in January.
