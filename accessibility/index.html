Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 2026

Freedom Mobility NY is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience and applying relevant accessibility standards.

Our Approach

  • We follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines where feasible.
  • Our site uses semantic HTML, proper heading structure, ARIA labels, and sufficient color contrast.
  • Images include meaningful alternative text.
  • Forms are labeled and keyboard accessible.

Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some third-party content or older documents may not yet meet the highest standards. We are actively working to address these areas.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. Please let us know if you encounter any barriers:

  • Email: info@freedommobilityny.com
  • Phone: (585) 488-0771

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.
