Free Home Accessibility Consultation Across Upstate NY

Book a free in-home stairlift, ramp, or vertical lift consultation with Freedom Mobility — locally owned, serving Rochester, Buffalo, and Syracuse NY.

Updated September 8, 2026 · Rochester · Buffalo · Syracuse

If stairs, a porch, or a split-level entry are limiting someone you love, the next step does not have to be a hard sell. Freedom Mobility offers a free in-home consultation for families across Rochester, Buffalo, and Syracuse.

Who this is for

  • Adult children helping a parent age in place safely
  • Seniors who want to stay in their own home longer
  • Caregivers and discharge planners looking for a local installer they can trust

What we install

  • Stairlifts for straight or curved staircases
  • Ramps for safe entry and porch access
  • Vertical platform lifts for wheelchair and power-chair access

Every new installation includes our 18-month Gold Star Service Warranty (terms apply).

What to expect on the visit

  1. We listen to how the home is used day to day.
  2. We measure the stairs, rise, or landing carefully.
  3. We explain realistic options and typical timelines.
  4. You get a clear next step — with no pressure to decide on the spot.

Serving Western and Central New York

We are locally owned and operated near Rochester, with regular work in Buffalo and Syracuse. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, with evenings and weekends by appointment.
